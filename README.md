
# UDYAAP - EduLocker App

**UDYAAP (formerly EduLocker)** is an educational technology concept focused on making academic information, student documents, learning resources, scholarships, and career opportunities more accessible through a unified digital platform.

The project envisions a connected ecosystem where students can organise their academic journey, discover relevant opportunities, access educational resources, and receive career guidance in one place. It aims to reduce the challenges caused by scattered information and limited access to guidance, especially for students in rural and underserved communities. UDYAAP represents a vision of using technology to help students learn, grow, and move forward with greater confidence.
